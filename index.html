+

What they fear
Burning another month of agent productivity on a vendor that doesn't hold
Long-term contracts
Low contact rates eroding agent morale
Compliance exposure from aggregated leads
Value Proposition
When the same lead goes to four to six agencies, the contact rate problem is structural. Keystone Platinum caps at two buyers. Pay as you go.
Lead Magnet
Case study PDF — buyer dropped CPA from $540 to $440, lifted sales 25% in one quarter. + 30-min strategy call.
02 Scaling U65 Call Center
+
30–200+ agents. Convoso or peer enterprise dialer in place. Building per-agent economics that hold at scale. Unit cost needs to work at 100 agents.
What they fear
Lead supply that does not scale with headcount
Compliance liability from sources that cannot be audited
Manual bid desks eating into operational bandwidth
Value Proposition
At 30+ agents the bid desk becomes a full-time job. Ride the Wave auto-bids on Platinum in real time. KeyStats shows per-day CPA without pulling from Convoso. Convoso and TruAlliant pre-wired.
Lead Magnet
Best Practices PDF + KeyStats walkthrough.
03 TCPA-Aware Agency
+
15–100 agents. Owner, GC, or compliance officer has seen TCPA enforcement up close. Aware that one bad campaign creates eight-figure exposure. Buying from aggregators with growing concern about consent provenance.
What they fear
A class action from leads bought in good faith from a vendor that cannot produce the consent record
Regulatory scrutiny post-MediaAlpha settlement
Value Proposition
After the August 2025 FTC settlement, the question buyers ask has changed. Every Keystone lead: landing page, form, consent language, timestamp, source URL — in one place, producible on request.
Lead Magnet
30-minute provenance walkthrough + lead quality case study.
04 OOEP-Active Agency
+
Sells STM, limited medical, fixed indemnity year-round. Runs Feb–Oct as active sales months. Current vendors ramp down after OEP closes in January.

CPA growing when supply tightens Feb–Oct
Value Proposition
Keystone generates U65 through OOEP because the buyer base sells private market plans year-round. The ad spend keeps running in February. Landing pages are live in August. Supply does not wind down because buyers do not wind down.
